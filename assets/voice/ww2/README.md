# WWII voices — Uncle Tan and Mei

Thirteen user-selected performances generated with Eleven v3 (Natural stability,
50%): eight Kelvin lines for Uncle Tan and five Lilian — Warm, Calm and Captivating
lines for Mei. The user selected and downloaded each take from the generated pairs.
Original download names and hashes are recorded in `lines.json` and `mei-lines.json`;
generation text includes acting cues, while `text` contains the game subtitles.

Imported audio is mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz / 128 kbps, normalized with two-pass
FFmpeg loudnorm to -21 LUFS / -2 dBTP. Playback uses the shared -3.6 dB dialogue
level and respects the chapter's Sound setting. Originals and measurement results
are retained locally under `artifacts/voice-originals/ww2/`.

Mei's call to get inside plays once at the start of the siren sequence. Other
clips follow their dialogue cards and stop when advanced or when leaving dialogue.
Sparky and the neighbour remain text-only. Missing audio falls back to subtitles.
These are fictional AI performances, not archival audio.
