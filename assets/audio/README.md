# Chapter ambience

These WAV files are original, deterministic synthesized effects generated by
`tools/build_soundscape.py`: fan motor/air, distant street rumble, indistinct
nonverbal crowd murmur, footsteps and a small aerial-fitting click. They contain
no third-party recordings or intelligible speech. They are illustrative sound
design, not archival recordings of Singapore in 1965.

The crowd fades out when the press conference starts. Fan/street levels reduce
under the original speech. Pause → Ambient sound toggles these effects, separately
from the archive's Mute button and Uncle Tan's voice controls.
