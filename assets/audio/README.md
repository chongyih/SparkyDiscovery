# Chapter ambience

These OGG files are trimmed, filtered and looped from free field recordings by
`tools/build_soundscape.py` (needs `numpy`, `scipy` and `soundfile`; sources are
cached in ignored `artifacts/audio-sources/`). They are modern recordings used as
illustrative sound design, not archival sound of Singapore in 1965.

The adapted koel and myna clips remain available under CC BY-SA 4.0. This does not
change the licence of other game files. The journal credits these recordists.

Levels: the broadcast plays at about −18 LUFS and Uncle Tan's voice at about −24.6 LUFS.
His clean modern recording sounds louder than the thin 1965 audio at equal loudness (see
`ARCHIVE_VOLUME_DB` and `VOICE_VOLUME_DB` in `game/interface.gd`). Beds are
built at about −22 LUFS and bird calls at −20 LUFS. `game/soundscape.gd` then places the
walking mix about 20 dB below dialogue, lower still during dialogue, tuning and the broadcast.

The TV static plays while tuning. It gets quieter as the dial nears the station and cuts
out when the picture locks. It is puzzle feedback, so the ambient toggle does not mute it.

In game, the street, cicadas and fan are positional loops. Koel and myna calls come
from random trees every 14–32 seconds. The neighbours' murmur fades out when the press
conference starts, and the other beds duck under the original speech. Pause → Ambient
sound toggles all of these, separately from the archive's Mute button and Uncle Tan's
voice controls.
